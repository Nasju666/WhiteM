Opening Story (Emotional Hook – 1 minute)

Last year, our friend Kian wanted to buy a second-hand digital camera worth ₱3,000.

He found a seller on Instagram. The post looked legitimate. The photos were clear. The conversation felt normal.
He saved up his allowance. He trusted the seller.
So he sent the payment.

Minutes later — he was blocked.
The seller disappeared. The account vanished.
And just like that, ₱3,000 was gone.

(Pause.)

Unfortunately, Kian’s story is not unique.
Every day, students and online buyers experience scams when purchasing through social media platforms.

🔴 Problem Statement (1 minute)

The problem is simple:
Online trading through social media lacks protection.

There is:
- No escrow system
- No verified seller identity
- No structured dispute resolution
- No accountability

Because of this, trust in online transactions decreases.
And when trust decreases, digital economic participation also decreases.
Many people hesitate to sell or buy online because they fear being scammed.

🔵 Introduction of Solution (30 seconds)

To address this, we propose White Market Trading Hub — a secure digital trading platform built with Django and integrated with blockchain-based escrow protection.

Our system creates a safer environment for buying and selling both physical and digital goods.

🟣 System Overview (2 minutes)

📌 What Our Platform Offers

1️⃣ Verified Sellers
- Sellers must upload a valid ID
- Admin reviews and approves KYC
- Verified badge appears on seller profile
This discourages fake accounts.

2️⃣ Wallet-Based Authentication
- Users log in using crypto wallets such as MetaMask.
- No passwords stored
- Digital signature authentication
- Secure identity verification
The system never stores private keys.

3️⃣ Blockchain-Based Escrow
Here’s how transactions work:
- Buyer selects an item
- Buyer sends payment to escrow
- Funds are locked in a smart contract
- Seller delivers item
- Buyer confirms receipt
- Funds released automatically

If dispute is triggered:
- Funds are frozen
- Case moves to dispute resolution

4️⃣ Dispute Evaluation System
If buyer claims scam:
- Buyer submits: Reason, Screenshot proof, Description
- Seller submits: Counter evidence
- Admin reviews: Chat logs, Delivery proof, Transaction hash

Admin can:
- Release funds to seller
- Refund buyer
- Do a partial settlement
This protects BOTH buyer and seller.

5️⃣ Admin Dashboard
Using Django Admin, staff can:
- Approve seller verification
- Monitor transactions
- Handle disputes
- Suspend suspicious accounts
- View transaction history
